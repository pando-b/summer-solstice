# Positioning one-pager

Thirty minutes, not half a day. It feeds the Build Brief's direction and the price and channel decisions. It is skill-written: it summarizes evidence in your own words and cites evidence IDs, and it never pastes fetched text.

## The "who already automates this" probe

Before writing, answer one question: who, or what, already does this job for the persona today?

1. Search the shape's own channel (plugin directory, extension store, marketplace) for the job, and the open web for "<job> tool", "<job> automation", and "<job> API".
2. Check whether a general-purpose assistant or agent can already do the job well from a one-line request. If it can, and the product would own no workflow or data, the candidate is a thin wrapper: fail the channel check with that reason.
3. List each incumbent with its price and one proof of traction (installs, reviews, a published customer count). Save any number through `solstice demand fetch`; save listings as evidence.

## The page

```
POSITIONING — <working name>
Competitive alternatives: <what the persona uses today, including spreadsheets or doing nothing, and what it costs them>
Incumbents: <tool | price | traction proxy (evidence ID) | most common complaint theme>
Unique attributes: <what this product does that the alternatives do not>
Value: <what those attributes let the persona do, in their terms>
Best-fit customers: <who cares most about that value, and where they gather>
Market category: <the frame that makes the value obvious to them>
Price corridor: <floor (cheapest credible alternative) to ceiling (value anchor)>, with the evidence IDs
Agent angle: <can a personal agent do the core job through this product without a human in the browser?>
```

## Rules

- Positioning comes from the alternatives, not from the features you hope to build.
- "Works with agents" alone is not a unique attribute unless the persona wants that and the incumbents cannot offer it.
- Price the pro forma at the corridor floor, not the ceiling.
- If no unique attribute survives the probe, say so. That is a finding, and the channel or spend check usually fails with it.
